## 🕹️ About

Transport Fever 3 is a transport management and economic simulation game from Urban Games, published by Paradox Interactive. Build and manage sprawling networks across land, sea, and air, connect growing cities, move passengers and cargo, and shape more than a century of transportation history. The game is scheduled to launch on September 29, 2026, and this beta test gives selected players the chance to help refine the experience while receiving a free game key.

Transport Fever 3 is for strategy, simulation, city-building, and logistics fans who enjoy solving complex systems and creating expansive, highly detailed networks.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Dynamic Worlds** — Develop transport networks across temperate, desert, tropical, and sub-arctic environments.
- 🚆 **Complete Transportation** — Operate railways, roads, shipping routes, and air connections across a living world.
- 📦 **Economic Simulation** — Deliver goods, move people, manage industries, and respond to changing regional demands.
- 🏙️ **Growing Cities** — Transform small settlements into thriving cities through efficient infrastructure.
- ⏳ **Transport History** — Progress through more than 120 years of evolving vehicles, industries, and architecture.
- 🛠️ **Tycoon and Sandbox Modes** — Pursue strategic goals or freely design the transport empire of your dreams.
- 🎯 **Campaign Challenges** — Take on historical missions that test your planning, logistics, and management skills.
- 🔧 **Modding Support** — Customize your experience with extensive modding options and cross-platform support.
